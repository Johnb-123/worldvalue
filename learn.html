Learning path

One way to work through value investing, step by step.

  1. Learn to read the accounts
    Start with the SEC beginners' guide on the Research links page, then the dictionary entries for book value, net debt and EBITDA.
  2. Price versus value
    The Intelligent Investor, then The Little Book of Value Investing for a short version.
  3. Margin of safety and risk
    The Most Important Thing and the free Oaktree memos (Research links page).
  4. Valuing a company
    The Little Book of Valuation, then the free NYU Stern valuation class (Research links page).
  5. Checking the numbers are real
    Financial Shenanigans: how accounts can flatter profits.
  6. Going deeper
    Security Analysis and Value Investing: From Graham to Buffett and Beyond.
